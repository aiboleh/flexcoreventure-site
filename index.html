Our journeyPerjalanan kami

From one Bitcoin to five companies.Daripada satu Bitcoin kepada lima syarikat.

  1. 2014

    The GenesisPermulaan

    The roots of Flexcore Venture trace back to 2014 when we took a pioneering step into the virtual realm, acquiring our first Bitcoin. This marked our initiation into the dynamic world of virtual assets, setting the stage for a journey defined by foresight and innovation.Akar Flexcore Venture bermula pada 2014 apabila kami melangkah ke dunia maya dengan memperoleh Bitcoin pertama kami. Ia menandakan permulaan kami dalam dunia aset maya yang dinamik, membuka jalan kepada perjalanan yang ditandai oleh pandangan jauh dan inovasi.

  2. 2016 – 2019

    Navigating CryptocurrencyMengemudi Mata Wang Kripto

    In the subsequent years, from 2016 to 2019, we seized the opportunities presented by the burgeoning cryptocurrency landscape. Diversifying our portfolio, we strategically invested in various cryptocurrencies, laying the groundwork for our expertise in the digital financial frontier.Dari 2016 hingga 2019, kami merebut peluang dalam landskap mata wang kripto yang sedang berkembang. Kami mempelbagaikan portfolio dengan melabur secara strategik dalam pelbagai mata wang kripto, sekali gus membina kepakaran kami dalam kewangan digital.

  3. 2019

    Techcryption MediaTechcryption Media

    In 2019, the culmination of our cryptocurrency journey led to the founding of Techcryption Media Sdn Bhd. This venture served as a testament to our commitment to fostering education and awareness about blockchain technology and the adoption of Bitcoin. Techcryption Media became a beacon for crypto enthusiasts, providing comprehensive coverage of news and information. Techcryption Media has since been wound down.Pada 2019, perjalanan kripto kami membawa kepada penubuhan Techcryption Media Sdn Bhd, sebagai bukti komitmen kami terhadap pendidikan dan kesedaran tentang teknologi blockchain dan penggunaan Bitcoin. Techcryption Media menjadi rujukan peminat kripto dengan liputan berita dan maklumat yang menyeluruh. Techcryption Media telah pun ditutup.

  4. 2020

    Tuah LandTuah Land

    The year 2020 witnessed the establishment of Tuah Land Sdn Bhd, marking our foray into the real estate industry. Specializing in the buying and selling of subsale houses and land, Tuah Land became an integral part of our diversified investment strategy, contributing to the dynamic expansion of our portfolio.Tahun 2020 menyaksikan penubuhan Tuah Land Sdn Bhd, menandakan langkah kami ke dalam industri hartanah. Dengan pengkhususan dalam jual beli rumah dan tanah subsale, Tuah Land menjadi sebahagian penting strategi pelaburan kami yang pelbagai.

  5. 2022 – 2023

    NFT ExplorationPenerokaan NFT

    Building on our foundation, we embraced the future by expanding our investments into the realm of Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) from 2022 to 2023. This strategic move reflected our unwavering commitment to staying at the forefront of transformative technologies and trends.Berpaksikan asas itu, kami meluaskan pelaburan ke dalam Token Tidak Boleh Ditukar Ganti (NFT) dari 2022 hingga 2023, mencerminkan komitmen kami untuk sentiasa berada di barisan hadapan teknologi dan trend yang transformatif.

  6. Q3 2023

    Birth of Flexcore VentureKelahiran Flexcore Venture

    The third quarter of 2023 marked a significant milestone in our journey as we founded Flexcore Venture. Driven by the vision to channel our resources into tangible, real-world industries, we made a strategic shift into sectors such as Fintech, Food Security, Property, Healthcare, E-commerce, Agritech, Edtech, Clean Energy and more. Flexcore Venture was born to be a catalyst for genuine business growth.Suku ketiga 2023 menjadi titik penting apabila kami menubuhkan Flexcore Venture. Didorong oleh visi untuk menyalurkan sumber kami ke dalam industri dunia sebenar, kami beralih secara strategik ke sektor seperti Fintech, Keselamatan Makanan, Hartanah, Penjagaan Kesihatan, E-dagang, Agriteknologi, Teknologi Pendidikan, Tenaga Bersih dan banyak lagi. Flexcore Venture lahir sebagai pemangkin pertumbuhan perniagaan yang sebenar.

  7. 2023

    Building the PortfolioMembina Portfolio

    In 2023 we invested in CHIP IN, a Malaysian digital finance platform, and The Komunal Cafe.   8. 2024

    Team VamosTeam Vamos

    In 2024 we invested in Team Vamos, a Malaysian esports organisation that competes in MPL Malaysia, Free Fire and PUBG. It marked our first step into esports and gaming.Pada 2024 kami melabur dalam Team Vamos, organisasi e-sukan Malaysia yang bertanding dalam MPL Malaysia, Free Fire dan PUBG. Ia langkah pertama kami ke dunia e-sukan dan permainan.

  9. 2026

    A.I. BolehA.I. Boleh

    A.I. Boleh is our venture in artificial intelligence. It teaches Malaysians to make videos with AI through short films, tutorials and the e-book Prompt Macam Director. In 2026 A.I. Boleh began building its next product, the Virtual Office: a team of AI workers that helps businesses get work done. It is in development, with a public demo coming soon.A.I. Boleh ialah usaha niaga kami dalam kecerdasan buatan. Ia mengajar rakyat Malaysia menghasilkan video dengan AI melalui filem pendek, tutorial dan e-book Prompt Macam Director. Pada 2026 A.I. Boleh mula membina produk seterusnya, Virtual Office: satu pasukan pekerja AI yang membantu perniagaan menyiapkan kerja. Ia masih dalam pembangunan, dengan demo awam akan datang.
